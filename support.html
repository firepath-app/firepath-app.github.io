Frequently asked questions

Is my financial data private?

Yes. All data stays on your device and syncs privately across your own Apple devices via your personal iCloud account. We don't have a server that stores your financial information, we don't sell data, and there's no account to create with us.

How do I sync FIRE Path across my iPhone and iPad?

Make sure you're signed into the same iCloud account on each device and that iCloud Drive is enabled in Settings. FIRE Path syncs automatically in the background — no setup required inside the app.

Why do I need a Finnhub API key for RSU stock prices?

Live stock prices for RSU grants come from Finnhub's free API. Since FIRE Path doesn't run its own servers, you connect your own free Finnhub key in Settings so price lookups happen directly between your device and Finnhub — nothing passes through us.

Is FIRE Path really free? What's the catch?

FIRE Path is free with no subscription and no in-app purchases. There's no catch — it was built out of a personal need for a better FIRE planning tool, and we'd rather people use it than paywall it.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → scroll to the bottom → "Erase All Data." This permanently removes everything stored on your device (and from iCloud, if sync is enabled). This can't be undone, so consider it a hard reset.

I found a bug or have a feature request. What do I do?

Email firepathappofficial@gmail.com with as much detail as you can — what you expected, what happened instead, and your device/iOS version if it's a bug. Feature requests are genuinely read and considered for future updates.

Does the AI assistant send my financial data anywhere?

The AI assistant runs on-device using Apple's on-device intelligence, so your financial details are not sent to an external server to generate responses.
